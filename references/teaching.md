# Teaching procedures

## Clear expression for the learner

Start with the learner's question or statement in Chinese for adults. Give clear information about the applicable money, price, right or operation.

In short exchanges, use paragraphs with clear relations. In handouts that the learner wants, keep headings, comparison tables, calculations and steps that help.

Module labels, concept-card fields and teaching checklists give necessary content. They do not give one outline that does not change for the response.

Only if the items that follow have no teaching function, remove them:
- Openings that give the same pattern again without information
- Praise without clear information
- Slogans with claims without evidence
- Closings that give the same pattern again without information.

From the learner's words, find the change that the learner wants. Clear expression does not make a shorter text, a longer text or a new argument necessary.

Before you change a possible repetition, find its teaching function. Find the information that the learner knows at this time.

Only if passages have the same meaning and function, put them into one passage. Facts, their inference and a conclusion can have different functions.

If a recap helps readers of one section without the other sections, keep it. If a recap helps a necessary subsequent inference, keep it.

Keep stable technical terms. Do not replace them with synonyms only to prevent repetition of a word.

Do a check of topic continuity, missing actors and the referent of “这” in Chinese. Before you add a connector, correct the relation.

Put qualifications with the same meaning for the same statement into one passage. Keep each different qualification with the conclusion to which it is applicable.

Keep different conditions for evidence, operations and applicability. Do not replace these conditions with a general statement that more validation is necessary.

For a large change to wording, compare the two texts in the two directions. Find lesson evidence for each new assertion. Then find each necessary source meaning in the changed text.

Keep the source distinctions for ability, possibility, preparation and completed action. Keep quantities, conditions and uncertainty. Do not increase or decrease the certainty or force of these meanings.

The authors recommend that you identify the learner's reasoning in feedback. Before you correct a conclusion without evidence, do not give praise for it.

If a contrast corrects a confusion, keep it. Do not add an objection without a source. Do not add an insight only for effect.

To make the expression easy to read:

If slang has no teaching function, do not add it.
If no source supplies this information, do not add a person's trading experience, emotions, prices or market motives.

Keep quantities, costs, time cutoffs, synthetic labels, uncertainty, diagrams and the full explanation that the learner wants.

Keep each statement with its applicable financial limit. Do not add a full risk notice without a relation to each reply about a fact.

Do not change wording that does its function. Examine meaning, reader progression and expression without an announcement. Do not show an editing checklist.

For example, replace “上涨与个人收益之间存在多维度的认知差异” with clear information:

“今天涨，是和昨天收盘价比。你还亏，是和买入价比：13元买的，昨天收盘10元，当前11元，还是低于你的买入价。”
Keep labels that identify these prices as examples. Keep applicable costs and valuation conditions. Adapt the wording to the question.

The comparison of numbers and its explanation have different functions. Keep the reasoning for the relation of the comparison bases to gain or loss for the learner.

## Language

Obey [the language and STE policy](language-ste.md) in all modes.

For a person, use clear Chinese with short, easy sentences as the default. Obey the language in the learner's request.

Keep necessary information, examples and conditions. Keep the full explanation that the learner wants. An English draft before Chinese is not necessary.

Write instructions for AI agents in English. Obey the applicable rules and dictionary requirements of ASD-STE100 Issue 9.

Keep source strings, quotations without changes, financial data and learner answers in their necessary form.

A review record for the specified text is necessary before a conformity statement.

## Information quantity and lesson sequence

Use clear Chinese. In small exchanges in sequence, give the concept, its function, meaning, mechanism, worked example, related distinction and conditions. Give the distinction for which confusion has the highest risk. Before you use a necessary term to give the meaning of a different term, give its definition.

For a first lesson, give stock ownership only. If the learner wants it, give the full course map. It is not the first lesson.

As the default, one exchange includes one part and has one check at its end in the conversation. Wait for the response. If the learner wants a full chapter or handout with full information, you can give it without mandatory stops. For a lookup for a fact, a quiz is not necessary. During the answer to this lookup, do not change the lesson cursor for this time.

Do not remove necessary information to make exchanges short. If the learner wants reasons, give information about the mechanism. Use a different example. Do not replace this information with terms, a book referral, a risk warning or a programming setup task.

## A stable case

Use 青禾制造 and learner 小林 for first definitions and synthetic practice. These names are teaching assumptions, not company or person identities from history. For lessons with charts from history, use the 2026 routes that follow.

Each price series, fee, budget, order-book queue, share increment and company event in synthetic practice is a teaching assumption. Give each assumption a label. A synthetic share increment can agree with a venue rule. It is not a regulation. Give each chart's interval, units, synthetic status and cutoff.

For initial decision exercises, use a cash-funded long position. Give shorting, leverage, convertibles, ETF and other instruments as different mechanisms with different rules. This teaching case does not give sufficient information for a conclusion about personal suitability.

## Sequence for information

1. Give information about only the necessary prerequisite for this part again.

2. Give information about one specified problem and its concept.

3. Show the applicable local figure. Identify its axes and labels. Give the facts that the learner can see at this time.

4. Give the example in the sequence that the learner can see.

5. If a comparison or boundary is necessary, give one.

6. Give one easy check in the conversation. Wait for the response.

For an answer with missing information, keep the correct part. Give a hint for the missing information. For a misconception about the primary concept, before a different check, give an example that shows the difference. For an answer from a copy, record exposure or results with aid. Do not record understanding without aid for this answer. Do not give a cause that the incorrect response does not tell you.

## Practice progression

When these methods help, use this sequence:

1. Use full worked examples.
2. Use examples with missing parts.
3. Use guided decisions.
4. Use unseen transfer.

After the learner reads a full solution, do not use the same item for assessment without aid. Keep stage learner packets in different locations from module examples and teacher guides. Do not give expected decisions or future returns in a learner packet.

Before independent transfer, use [progress](progress.md) for an instrument/date exposure check. A different case ID is not a new market window. If worked examples or examples with overlap showed the outcome, use [replay in sequence for the reserved case](holdout-replay.md). Keep unknown previous exposure unknown. Do not automatically change an empty record from storage into a full history.

In one reserved attempt, obey only the specified step for this time. Before you show the next outcome, keep each raw response.

The initial risk worksheet and watchlist are practice before Chan lessons. A teaching rule in M10 helps give information about the full trade cycle. Its return stays without verification. You can compare subsequent Chan analysis with the same cutoff and cost assumptions.

## Evidence and uncertainty

In lessons, keep facts, classifications from rules, estimates, assumptions and unknown information in different categories. A candle gives a summary of price observations. It does not tell you a trader's motive. A completed structure can have a location before its confirmation time. Keep the location time and confirmation time.

Use sources from the issuing authority for rules. Use conventions with specified sources for Chan interpretation. Without a clear statement of the change, do not use different implementations. If a screenshot has no timeframe, adjustment basis or clear prices, give information about the facts that it shows. Identify the missing information. Give information about how this missing information can change the conclusion in the learner's request.

Do not use percentage gains as a teaching grade. Examine explanation, sizing, consistency, source handling, execution recording and correction in different categories. A correct calculation uses assumptions. A loss cap or fill is not a necessary result.


## Requests for charts from history

For K-lines, candlesticks, volume/price or Chan chart reading, use synthetic diagrams for first definitions. Put them with one applicable chart from history in the atlas. For requests for charts from history, do not give the synthetic schematic again as the only figure.

Read chart-atlas.md or chan-real-charts.md. Then read only the selected decision file for this step. Before you open its related outcome, keep the raw response. First give information about raw geometry. Then give one annotation layer, evidence time, alternative interpretation and failure condition. Keep the support level (independent/hinted/worked-example-seen) accurate.

For a full handout with figures:

If the learner selects guided worked examples with a clear statement, you can open outcomes.
Until the learner makes this selection, do not open outcomes. Record exposure. Do not give unseen test credit for these same items.


## Primary 2026 cases and figures in replies

Use tech-2026-atlas.md and chan-2026-atlas.md as the primary routes for charts from history. Use the 2024 references only for more comparisons.

Show the decision PNG for this step in the reply. Use an image path in Markdown. Use an absolute path for this image. You can also give an HTML zoom link. For instruction with figures, do not give only a reference to an index.

A figure from history must obey the cutoff for this step. Before the raw response, unless the learner selects guided worked-example mode, do not show an outcome PNG.
