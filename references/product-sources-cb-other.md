# Convertible bonds and other listed funds: primary sources

The public release date is the publication date of the source document. The effective date is the date when its specified terms start. The intraday availability time is the first time during the day when the information was available. A date with day precision does not give this time.

Source check date: **2026-10-20**, Asia/Shanghai. Scope: mainland SSE/SZSE public convertible bonds, listed funds, exchange money funds and public REITs. This registry does not include all information about these products. It is not a full legal opinion or a broker operation manual.

  Publication, legal effectiveness, observation date and availability are different fields. Unless a statement gives a different precision, the dates that follow have day precision. Intraday times of initial release stay **unknown**.

This registry contains worked-event outcomes. The source check date is the same for all entries.

Tutor: Use the registry for source checks and guided explanations. In a learner packet for this step, do not show entries for product changes after this step's cutoff. Do not show the full reports to which these entries refer. Before you use rules for the registry observation date for previous decisions, do an effective-date check.

## cb-sse — SSE convertible trading

- Authority: SSE, Convertible Bond Trading Implementation Rules, March 2025 revision. [Notice and rule attachment](https://www.sse.com.cn/lawandrules/sselawsrules2025/bond/trading/currency/c/c_20250606_10781040.shtml). Public release date and effective date: **2025-03-28**. The June URL is a result of site migration. It does not give the date of legal effectiveness.
- Applicable to: matching trades in public-issue convertible bonds. Primary articles: 5–6, 13, 15–17. The full-price quote is for each CNY 100 face. Each bond has CNY 100 face. The tick is CNY 0.001. The matching order quantity is CNY 1,000 face or multiples.

  Same-day resale is permitted.

  The first-day limits are +57.3%/-43.3%. The limit for subsequent days is ±20%. First-day temporary halts and valid-order ranges are more constraints.
- Checks necessary:

- Examine suspension for the specified product.
- Examine account permissions.
- Examine fees.
- Examine the calendar applicable to product changes at their dates.

Negotiated/block transactions have different rules.

## cb-sz — SZSE convertible trading and lifecycle

- Authority: SZSE, 2025 revised rules collection in 深证上〔2025〕223号. [SZSE consolidated PDF](https://docs.static.szse.cn/www/lawrules/rule/all/W020250328578470423581.pdf). Public release date and effective date: **2025-03-28**. Document pages 325–336: Guideline 15. Pp. 407 and subsequent pages: convertible trading rules.
- Applicable to: 123107 public convertible. Trading Articles 5–6, 13, 15–17: the full-price quote is for each CNY 100 face. The tick is 0.001. The buy increment is CNY 1,000 face. A sale must include the full odd balance of face value.

  Same-day resale is permitted. The limit for subsequent days is ±20%.

  The first day has special valid-price bounds 56.7%–157.3% of issue price and halts. Do not give SZSE first-day limits as the statutory daily-limit formulation for SSE.
- Lifecycle: Guideline Articles 11, 15, 21–22, 28–29:

  The next trading day is the first permitted day for trades in converted unrestricted stock. Revision/call pre-warning is necessary five trading days before the expected trigger. The board decision is necessary at the trigger day. Disclosure is necessary at the next opening. The call trigger-to-redemption interval is 15–30 trading days. The no-call restriction is three months or more.

  For put and payment dates, the notice for the specified put and payment is necessary.
- Unknown: intraday notice availability, account eligibility at the decision time, fees, settlement/withdrawal and the thresholds in each bond's contract.

## cb-sse-events — SSE lifecycle dates and times

- Authority: SSE Guideline 12, March 2025 revision. [Notice for the registry check date](https://www.sse.com.cn/lawandrules/sselawsrules2025/bond/convertible/listing/c/c_20250516_10787557.shtml). Public release date and effective date: **2025-03-28**. Read the DOCX attachment.
- Applicable to: SSE issuers. Articles 12, 16, 18, 22–23, 27–28: conversion-stock timing: next trading day. Revision/call warning is necessary five trading days before the expected trigger. The board decision is necessary at the trigger day. Disclosure is necessary at the next opening.

  If the decision/disclosure is missing, exercise is not possible for the occurrence.

  For a call, reminders each day before registration are necessary.

  The trigger-to-cash-payment interval is 15–30 trading days. The no-call restriction is three months or more.

  A put notice is necessary before the next opening after the trigger. Reminders are necessary each day. The application starts in 15 trading days or less.

  For changed use of proceeds, the put-right deadline is 20 trading days or less after shareholder approval. Three notices or more are necessary. Conversion-end reminders and trading-stop rules are different deadlines.
- Wording check: do not replace SZSE's redemption-day wording with SSE's cash-payment wording. The alternatives for this bond are those in the bond's notice.

## fund-trading — General fund trades, 2026 rules

- SSE: [notice for the 2026 Trading Rules](https://www.sse.com.cn/lawandrules/sselawsrules2025/stocks/exchange/c/c_20260424_10816482.shtml). Public release date: **2026-04-24**, effective date **2026-07-06**. These rules are the legal replacement for the 2023 rules. Articles 3.1.4–5, 3.3.8–13: the buying increment is 100 units in the standard auction procedure. A sale must include the full odd remainder. The fund tick is CNY 0.001.

  The default daily limit is 10%, with exceptions in the special provisions.

  For listed money funds, same-day resale is permitted. For closed-period funds/REITs without a special provision, fund status does not make the exception applicable.
- SZSE: [2026 Trading Rules](https://docs.static.szse.cn/www/lawrules/rule/trade/current/W020260424690713155663.pdf), same publication/effective dates. Articles 3.1.4–5, 3.3.11–13: the fund tick is 0.001. Listed money funds have same-day resale. The default limit is 10%. For the specified ETF/LOF classes, the limit is 20%.
- [SZSE fund operation rules](https://docs.static.szse.cn/www/lawrules/rule/fund/W020220610536157903323.pdf), public release date and effective date: **2022-06-10**, Articles 8, 13, 18: the auction increment is 100 units. Exchange trading and subscription/redemption are different operations.
- Unknown: special-product limits for each day, deferred clauses, order phase/type, cancellation, broker fees and withdrawal times. **Before you give 506006 the default limit of 10%, do its special-rule check**.

## Before you use source information

For synthetic worked examples, give all prices, fees, dates, permissions and cash-processing assumptions. Notices from the identified source show only the fields they contain. Without other evidence, an OHLC file with day bars cannot give sufficient information for these items:

- Availability of the applicable facts
- Conversion eligibility
- Share entitlement
- NAV publication
- Fill price. For intraday decisions, keep the intraday availability time of a date-only announcement unknown. Only after a source check gives an availability cutoff independently of the date-only label, use the announcement. Full reports available at the registry check date can show outcomes after the previous cutoff of a learner packet.
