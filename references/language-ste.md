# Language and STE Policy

## Reader and language

Write instructions for AI agents and model tasks in English.

Use ASD-STE100 Issue 9 for this English prose.

Obey all applicable writing rules and dictionary requirements.

Before you give a conformity statement for the text, complete a review of this text. Keep the review record.

A policy, format check or short sentence does not give sufficient information for a conclusion about conformity.

If review evidence is missing, record the result as `unresolved`. Continue teaching that helps, without a conformity statement.

As the default, use clear Chinese with short, easy sentences for a person. This default includes explanations, questions, feedback, plans, progress and reports.

Keep the explanation, examples, conditions and qualifications necessary for understanding. Keep the full explanation that the learner wants.

Do not make literary Chinese mandatory. Do not make sentences with missing parts mandatory. Do not make a mandatory pattern for headings or lists.

Do not make Chinese obey English grammar, sentence limits or paragraph patterns.

An English draft before a Chinese response is not necessary. Write Chinese directly from the task and source meaning.

Examine Chinese for meaning and clear relations. Make sure that it is easy to read.

Do not change actors, actions, causality, timing, quantities, units, conditions, exceptions, negation, scope, modality or uncertainty.

Keep code, commands, paths, identifiers, fixed values, quotations without changes, source data and learner answers in their necessary form.

A model reader does not make translation of source data necessary.

New instructions in code or quotations must also obey the English writing requirements.

For the content that the user wants, first obey the language in the user's request.

References to Chinese teaching in course documents give the default. If the learner gives a language in the request, adapt the prose that gives information.

Before the lesson that the learner wants, do not add a language announcement or writing lesson.

## Standard and review evidence

Use [ASD-STE100 Issue 9](https://www.asd-ste100.org/assets/files/ASD-STE100_ISSUE9.pdf), with its writing rules and dictionary.

For applicability, use the rules, exceptions and dictionary of the official standard. This policy does not replace these sources.

For a conformity review, identify the text and its source version or hash.

Record rule applicability, dictionary senses and forms, and evidence for technical terms.

Keep meaning review, English conformity review and Chinese expression review in different categories.

If necessary evidence is missing, give no conformity statement for this scope. If the text does not obey an applicable rule, give no conformity statement for this scope.

Previous review evidence does not give sufficient information for a conclusion about conformity of subsequently changed text.

If the user wants a review or a condition changes the answer, include the applicable review information. If no condition changes the answer, use the next rule. Unless the user wants a review, keep review work in different records from teaching replies.

This policy contains all necessary instructions. No other installed writing skill is necessary.
