# Assessment routes

For S06, use only the learner packet for this step in [product practice](product-practice.md). ETF, LOF, CB, REIT, MONEY and CLOSED each have four steps. Before you open the related teacher packet, keep the raw response for this step. While responses are missing, do not read the full guide for the product stage. All six tracks are assisted practice. They are not unseen transfer tests.

After you keep all responses for the selected track, use the [teacher guide for the product stage](assessment/product-stage.md). Examine these items in different categories:
- Product rights
- Valuation availability
- Rules for the selected operation
- No-trade reasoning
- Order/event state
- Net results after reconciliation. An attempt that is not completed or has profit does not give sufficient information for a conclusion about competence.

Before the learner gives an answer, read only the selected learner packet from [exercise index](exercises/index.md). For an assessment without aid, do not read its teacher guide or module examples with solutions. If the learner wants a hint, before you give the hint, set the item to `guided`.

Before selection, do an [instrument/date exposure](progress.md) check. Case IDs do not give sufficient information for a conclusion about independence. Price observations and future prices from feedback are exposure in all routes for price charts and Chan charts.

For example, T26-13 feedback shows all five future bars of T26-15. The subsequent T26-14 prefix contains these bars from feedback. After access to T26-13 feedback or the T26-14 prefix, do not give unseen test credit for T26-15. Keep missing history unknown.

For S05 transfer in sequence, use [the different reserve for H01](holdout-replay.md). The replay for price charts and strategy replay are teaching routes with solutions. These two routes are not the default S05 assessment without previous exposure. H01 has a different synthetic instrument, price sequence, account reports and three prefix files. Only after an exposure check, select H01.

In the same attempt, the specified previous prefix and related feedback let the attempt continue. They do not give sufficient information for a conclusion about three market samples with statistical independence. Exposure from different cases, previous attempts or events without this exception prevents independent credit.

If H01 has previous exposure or history is unknown, use it as assisted/unknown practice. Give a report of the missing assessment evidence. Do not make an unseen label with a different case name.

The 2024 atlases for price charts and Chan charts that give more examples are assisted demonstrations. This includes links from modules. Their topic labels or kept pages can help recognition. Do not give identification credit without aid from these routes.

Use the neutral prefix questions in H01 for the different transfer check. H01 includes a finite task for M11 about grouping and connection. It does not give sufficient information for a conclusion about high-level Chan construction.

Before you read the full teacher guide for a stage, keep all raw responses for this stage. For one answer at a time, give feedback only about this question with its data and definitions from previous lessons. While subsequent items stay without aid, do not read the full answer set for the stage. If the learner wants the full solution, change the remaining items to guided practice with a clear statement.

After you keep the full set of raw responses for the stage, read the related teacher guide:

- [Stage 01: market, account, risk and selection](assessment/stage-01.md)

- [Stage 02: quotes, candles and context](assessment/stage-02.md)

- [Stage 03: full rule and verification](assessment/stage-03.md)

- [Stage 04: Chan structure and timing](assessment/stage-04.md)

- [Stage 05: cycles of decisions](assessment/stage-05.md).

For H01, before you open a teacher JSON with a number, keep the raw response with this number. Open only this JSON in `references/assessment/holdout-replay/`. Until this feedback, do not open the next prefix. The guide for the full stage does not replace this gate for each packet.

For the strategy-aligned finite case, after the raw response for this step, read only its guide with the same number. The guides are [01](assessment/strategy-case-01.md), [02](assessment/strategy-case-02.md), [03](assessment/strategy-case-03.md), [04](assessment/strategy-case-04.md), [05](assessment/strategy-case-05.md), [06](assessment/strategy-case-06.md). Do not open a guide with a subsequent number.

[full summary of the strategy case](assessment/strategy-case.md) is available only with one of these conditions:
- You kept all six raw responses
- You changed the full remaining case to guided worked-example mode with a clear statement.

For feedback for a previous packet without aid, do not use this summary.

Examine reasoning with evidence, assumptions, arithmetic, information timing and uncertainty that the learner records. Keep process quality, strategy evidence and outcome in different categories. A profit is not automatic success. A rule-consistent loss is not automatic failure. Rule consistency does not give sufficient information to make sure that a strategy is satisfactory.

For each part with missing understanding, give a small explanation or hint. Give a new transfer item. Do not only give the initial answer again for memorization. Record the initial response and changed response in different entries. Do not make these graduation rules mandatory for all learners:
- A profit streak
- A fixed number of trades
- Trading with money in a broker account.
